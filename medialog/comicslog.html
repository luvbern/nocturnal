Comics Log

I'm usually drawn to characters who are the complete opposite of me—strong, smart, confident, a little flirty, and capable of going after what they want.

One thing I can't stand is when a character is written as stupid or overly clingy. Even worse is when they starts off with a strong personality and good judgment, but somewhere along the way the author completely changes their character. Like, what happened?! (╯°□°)╯︵ ┻━┻ It instantly kills my interest. Idc if I'm already on Chapter 90—if the character suddenly becomes dumb for the sake of the plot, I'm dropping it lol.
